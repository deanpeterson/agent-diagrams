OpenShift AI: Data Science Pipelines orchestrate, MLflow observes

The pipeline runs the training and gates pass/fail; the training step streams its metrics to MLflow live — two complementary services

orchestration — DSP runs generate → train → evaluate live metrics — train_gd logs to MLflow each epoch learned — MLflow loss curve · the evaluate gate

One runs it, the other watches it learn. Data Science Pipelines executes the DAG as Argo pods and gates success — the evaluate step fails the run unless the model recovers w≈2, b≈1, so a green run provably learned. While it runs, the train_gd step streams its metrics (loss, w, b) to MLflow every epoch — authenticated with the pipeline’s own identity — and MLflow draws the live loss curve. Two complementary OpenShift AI services, surfaced in the same dashboard.
